About Our School

Our school offers training in traditional bobbin lace for complete beginners and advanced enthusiasts. We teach various techniques, create decorations, jewelry, and classic lace.

Teaching takes place in small groups led by experienced instructors. On the website, you will find basic information about courses and registration options.

Course Offer

  • Basics of Bobbin Lace
  • Traditional Patterns
  • Jewelry from Lace
  • Christmas Decorations
  • Fine Lace
  • Children's Bobbin Lace
